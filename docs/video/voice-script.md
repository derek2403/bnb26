# MORE video: voice script, version 3

One line for each scene, in video order. Make one audio file for each line.
Name the files `01.mp3` to `33.mp3` and put them in
`docs/video/remotion/public/voice/`.

The text in square brackets is an ElevenLabs tag. The voice does not read it.

Version 2 has longer lines for the scenes that explain an idea. The script
has about 475 words. At a normal speed that is about 2 minutes 50 seconds,
so the scene lengths in the video must be fitted to the audio after it is
recorded.

| File | Scene | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | The stock market closes | [calm, serious] Friday. The stock market closes. |
| 02 | A Monday gap | [serious, concerned] On Monday the price opens far lower. A margin loan is liquidated before you can act. |
| 03 | MORE | [confident, bright] This is MORE. Margin on R W A exposure, without liquidation. |
| 04 | Every deposit is a choice | [clear, friendly] When you deposit a stock token, you choose what you get: a leveraged token that moves with the price, or a stable token with a fixed dollar value. |
| 05 | Alice deposits | [clear, friendly] Let's assume one Nvidia token costs a hundred dollars. Alice deposits one, and the pool mints her leveraged tokens. |
| 06 | Bob deposits | [clear, steady] Bob deposits one too and gets a fixed hundred dollars. The pool holds two shares, but only Alice takes the price moves. That is two times leverage. |
| 07 | The price rises | [upbeat] Nvidia rises ten percent. The pool is worth two hundred twenty. |
| 08 | Bob takes 100 | [clear, steady] Bob is repaid first, because he is the stable side, like a lender. He takes his hundred, and Alice gets the other one hundred twenty. |
| 09 | Alice gains 20% | [pleased, confident] The stock gained ten percent, but Alice gained twenty. After Bob is repaid, she gets two times the gain. That is her leverage. |
| 10 | The price falls | [curious, serious] What if the price falls? At ninety, the pool is one hundred eighty. |
| 11 | Bob takes 100 | [clear, steady] Bob still takes a hundred. Alice has eighty left. |
| 12 | At $50 | [serious] At fifty dollars, the whole pool pays Bob. |
| 13 | The formula | [clear, teaching] Leverage is the pool's stock, divided by Alice's part. |
| 14 | The leverage table | [clear, serious] As the price falls, Alice's part shrinks much faster than the pool. So her leverage climbs from two times to six. |
| 15 | Zero is not liquidation | [reassuring, firm] But zero does not mean liquidated. Her value is only the pool minus Bob's part. So when the price rises again, her tokens have value again. |
| 16 | The question | [curious] So how do we control the leverage? [confident] The total pool sells off some of its stock to bring it down. |
| 17 | Too high at $80 | [clear, steady] At eighty dollars, the leverage is too high. The pool brings it back to two times. |
| 18 | The first sale | [clear, steady] It sells half a share for forty dollars. The money stays in the pool as U S D C. |
| 19 | After the sale | [reassuring] Nobody lost value. Bob has a hundred. Alice has sixty. |
| 20 | The sale table | [clear, steady] As the price keeps falling, the pool sells four times. Each sale moves more value into dollars and resets the leverage. |
| 21 | At $40, before and after | [confident] At forty dollars without selling, Alice has nothing and Bob loses money. With selling, Bob is whole and Alice keeps something. |
| 22 | The buyback | [upbeat] When the price rises, the pool buys stock back. |
| 23 | The full trip | [clear, honest] Over the full trip, Bob stays at a hundred. Alice recovers, but not all the way. That is the cost of protection. |
| 24 | The algorithm | [confident] An algorithm does the buying and selling. If the leverage is too high, it sells stock. If it is low again, it buys back. |
| 25 | Why Alice joins | [confident] Users who want leverage take Alice's side: margin without liquidation, for a funding fee. |
| 26 | Loan against MORE | [firm] A loan has a liquidation price and a penalty. MORE has none. |
| 27 | Why Bob joins | [clear, honest] Lenders take Bob's side: a higher yield, for a higher risk. |
| 28 | Lending rates | [confident] That can pay more than lending on Binance, Lista, or Aave. |
| 29 | Create a market | [bright, inviting] MORE is not only a platform. It is a launchpad. Anyone can launch their own market: choose the stock, set the leverage range, the funding fee and the vault seed. Then anyone can join that market, as Alice or as Bob. |
| 30 | End card | [confident, warm] MORE. Margin on R W A exposure, without liquidation. |
